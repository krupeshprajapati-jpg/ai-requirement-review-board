# Payment and retry risks

- Automatic payment retries without a stated delay, retry limit, or stop condition can create confusing or repeated collection attempts. Clarify backoff, customer notification, and how a successful payment prevents later retries.
- A payment or balance change without a clear amount, effective date, or customer-visible confirmation leaves financial impact difficult to predict. State how the amount is calculated and what the customer can review before confirming.

# Irreversible and account changes

- Deleting, submitting, or changing an important account setting without confirmation or a recovery path can cause avoidable customer harm. Identify which actions are reversible and what confirmation is required.
- Updating a repayment account or contact detail without specifying verification, effective timing, and failure behavior can leave the customer unsure which value is active. Describe the verified state and how it is communicated.

# Customer communication

Missing status updates after a disputed transaction or failed action can lead to duplicate submissions and support contacts. Define a reference number, visible status, and a clear next step when the system cannot complete the request.
