# Loan and repayment terms

- **EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment):** A scheduled periodic repayment amount. A requirement should say whether fees, interest, and rounding affect the displayed or collected amount.
- **Tenure:** The remaining repayment duration, usually expressed as a number of instalments or months. State whether a change affects the EMI, the end date, or both.
- **Part-payment:** An additional payment against an outstanding loan balance outside the regular instalment. Clarify minimum and maximum amounts, when it can be made, and how it is applied.

# Identity and servicing terms

- **OTP (One-Time Password):** A short-lived code used to verify a user action. Specify its delivery channel, expiry, retry limits, and what happens after failed verification.
- **Repayment account:** The account used to collect scheduled payments. Requirements should distinguish selecting or verifying an account from changing the account used for a future collection.
- **Service request:** A customer-submitted case that can be tracked through a reference identifier and status. Define the information required to submit it and how updates are presented.
